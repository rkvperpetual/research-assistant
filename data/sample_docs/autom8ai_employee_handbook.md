# Autom8AI Employee Handbook

## Introduction

Welcome to Autom8AI! This handbook outlines the policies, benefits, and expectations for all team members.

## Leave Policy

### Annual Leave
- **Junior Engineer (L1–L2):** 15 vacation days per year
- **Mid-level Engineer (L3–L4):** 18 vacation days per year
- **Senior Engineer (L5–L6):** 22 vacation days per year
- **Principal / Staff (L7+):** 25 vacation days per year

### Sick Leave
All full-time employees are entitled to 10 paid sick days per calendar year. Sick days do not roll over.

### Parental Leave
- **Primary caregiver:** 16 weeks fully paid parental leave
- **Secondary caregiver:** 6 weeks fully paid parental leave
- Applies to biological, adoptive, and foster parents.
- Available after completing 6 months of service.

### Bereavement Leave
Up to 5 days of paid bereavement leave for immediate family (spouse, child, parent, sibling).
Up to 3 days for extended family.

### Unpaid Leave
Employees may request unpaid leave of up to 90 days with manager approval.

## Work Hours & Remote Work Policy

Autom8AI operates on a **flexible hours** model. Core collaboration hours are 10 AM–3 PM in the team's local timezone.
Employees may work fully remote unless their role explicitly requires on-site presence.

## Code of Conduct

1. Treat all colleagues with respect.
2. Protect confidential information.
3. Disclose any conflicts of interest to your manager immediately.
4. Do not use company resources for personal commercial activity.

## Performance Review Cycle

Performance reviews occur twice per year: in June and December.
- Engineers at L3 and above are eligible for discretionary bonuses based on company and individual performance.
- Promotion decisions are made during the December cycle.

## Security Policy

### Device Security
- All laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled.
- Screen lock must activate after 5 minutes of inactivity.
- Personal devices used for work must be enrolled in MDM.

### Data Handling
- Customer data must never be stored on personal devices.
- All production access requires MFA.
- Credentials must be stored in the approved password manager (1Password Business).

### Incident Reporting
Security incidents must be reported to security@autom8ai.com within 1 hour of discovery.
